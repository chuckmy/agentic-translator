# Style guide — News translation

## Audience
General readers familiar with the news domain but not specialist insiders.

## Register
Neutral-formal, informative, paced for skim-reading.

## Quotations
Translate quoted speech with attention to the speaker's register. Preserve any idiomatic flavor; do not paraphrase quotations into bland summary.

## Proper nouns
- Use established English/Japanese forms where they exist (e.g., 鈴鹿サーキット = Suzuka Circuit).
- For new or invented company/product names, transliterate or keep as-is.
- People's names: family-name-first in JA, given-name-first in EN, with conventional capitalization.

## Tone
- Avoid sensationalism.
- Preserve hedges ("may", "is expected to", "according to").
- Quotation attributions ("〜と語った") should not be flattened into bland "said" — match the register: "remarked", "noted", "expressed confidence", "framed the product as", etc.

## Length
- Match the source paragraph structure. Do not consolidate paragraphs.
- Within paragraphs, sentence-splitting is permitted if a target-language sentence would otherwise become unwieldy.

## What to avoid
- Editorial tone in factual reporting.
- Inventing details to flesh out terse source.
- Footnotes or in-text glosses (this is news, not academic prose).
