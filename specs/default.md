# Default Translation Specification

## Audience
General readers, no specialist background assumed.

## Register
Neutral, professional, accessible.

## Style preferences
- Prefer clear, concrete sentences over abstract phrasing.
- Keep paragraph structure of the source.
- Preserve proper nouns; do not invent equivalents.

## Things to avoid
- Adding information not in the source.
- Omitting hedges (e.g., "may", "appears to") that signal epistemic stance.
- Mechanical word-for-word translation that breaks target-language flow.
